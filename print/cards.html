Major Revisions: print and play

A deduction card game for scientists: find the flaw hidden in the manuscript before your review rounds run out. Version 0.1. 133 cards in standard poker size (63 × 88 mm), 9 per A4 page.

Reference3 cards1 pages
Studies8 cards1 pages

Print at 100% on A4 with background graphics on. For double-sided cards, print backs.html on the reverse.

The Author's crib sheet is the flaw key: every planted flaw, its verdict, the request and guideline item that catch it, and why it matters.

The five cards
  • Question What the study set out to answer, and what was planned.
  • Method How the study was designed and run.
  • Sample Who or what was studied, and who was lost.
  • Measurement How the outcomes and exposures were measured.
  • Analysis How the data were analysed and turned into a conclusion.
A review, step by step
  • 1. The Author picks a Study and plants one or two flawed cards among the five.
  • 2. The Author reads the claim and every card's As-submitted line.
  • 3. On your turn, play a Request, or file a report on one card.
  • 4. The Author reads aloud only the answer to that request.
  • 5. When rounds run out, every reviewer gives a verdict. Reveal all.
Scoring
  • Flawed card found +3
  • Sound card accused -1
  • Right verdict +2
  • Each round left when you find it +1
  • Author, per flaw that survives +3
